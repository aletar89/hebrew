This directory needs image files for the German reading app, following this naming convention:

Use German words as filenames with appropriate image extensions:
Examples: Apfel.png, Auto.jpg, Brot.webp, Zug.svg, etc.

Supported image formats: png, jpg, jpeg, gif, svg, webp, and other standard web image formats

The app supports all German letters and will automatically identify which letter the image belongs to based on the first character of the filename.

Examples:
- Apfel.png - A picture of an apple, starting with A
- Auto.jpg - A picture of a car, starting with A
- Brot.png - A picture of bread, starting with B
- Zug.webp - A picture of a train, starting with Z

The app will automatically detect available images and only use letters that have corresponding images.
You can add multiple images for each letter (just add more words starting with that letter), and the app will randomly select them during gameplay.

The images should be clear, child-friendly illustrations that a 4-year-old can easily recognize.
